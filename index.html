FAQ

Things people ask us.

Quick answers. If you don't see yours, reach out — we reply within 24h.

Is WAKE free?

Yes. Building, publishing, discovering and remixing Wakes is free. WAKE Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks PDF export, AI itinerary generation, full offline mode and more.

How does Live mode work?

When you start a Live, WAKE captures geo-tagged photo moments and threads them into your trip's timeline automatically. Friends and the community can follow along in real time.

Can I remix someone else's Wake?

Yes. Tap "Remix" on any public Wake and you get a private copy you can edit, customise and use as the base of your own trip. The original author gets a notification.

How do I become a Creator?

Open the app: Profile → Creator Program → Apply. Once approved you can publish premium signed itineraries and earn money on each sale.

What languages does WAKE support?

WAKE works in Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Romanian, Russian, Hindi, Chinese and Azerbaijani. Itineraries are auto-translated, so you write once and reach all travellers.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete account. All your data is removed within 30 days. We don't sell anything to third parties — promise.
