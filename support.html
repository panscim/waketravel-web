Common topics

  • Delete your account. Open the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. All your data is removed within 30 days.
  • Cancel Wake Pro subscription. iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → WAKE → Cancel. Refund requests are handled by Apple App Store.
  • Refund a Premium itinerary. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Apply to the Creator Program. Open the app: Profile → Creator Program → Apply.
  • Report a Wake, a user or a comment. Long-press the content → Report. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.
  • Lost access to your account. Use "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google" on the login screen if you originally registered with one of these providers.
